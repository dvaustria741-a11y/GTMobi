# ChatGPT image prompts (UI assets)

General rules to avoid generic AI look: ask for flat vector or screen-print style, a fixed
palette, hard-edged shapes, no text/letters inside the image (text is rendered in-engine),
no glossy gradients, no random extra elements, transparent or single-color background,
one asset per image. Generate icons as a grid on one sheet, then slice.

## 1. Menu background (sunset skyline)
"Flat screen-print illustration of a Miami-style waterfront skyline at dusk, seen from across
a bay. Limited palette: deep violet #2B1B4A, hot magenta #E5398C, orange #FF8A3D, teal #1FB5A8,
near-black navy. Hard-edged geometric buildings, thin searchlight beams, a few palm silhouettes.
Visible halftone grain. No text, no logos, no people, no lens flares. 2400x1080, landscape."

## 2. Settings icon sheet
"Sheet of 12 monochrome white UI icons on a transparent background, 4x3 grid, equal spacing,
consistent 4px stroke, rounded caps, 128x128 each: graphics, audio, controls, gameplay,
language, accessibility, controller, touch layout, brightness, data/storage, credits, back arrow.
Simple geometric line style. No text, no shadows, no gradients."

## 3. HUD frame pieces
"Set of minimal HUD elements, white on transparent: circular minimap frame, health bar and armor
bar (thin rounded rectangles), wanted-level star outline and filled star, ammo counter plate,
phone button. Flat, 2px strokes, slight chamfered corners. No text, no glow."

## 4. Map style reference
"Top-down stylized map texture of a subtropical island with wetlands, city grid, highways,
beaches. Flat colors: water #2E6CA8, land #E6E3A0, parks #7FC46B, roads #FFFFFF. No labels."
